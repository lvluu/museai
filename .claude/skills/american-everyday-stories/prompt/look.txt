PERIOD LOOK AND FILMING STYLE

Photorealistic live-action period drama, as if shot in the year stated above on 35 mm colour negative film with spherical lenses, then printed: warm natural colour with cream and amber highlights, soft powder-blue skies, brown-olive shadows, slightly lifted blacks, gently rolled-off highlights, fine natural film grain, mild halation around bright windows and chrome. Moderate saturation: rich rust, burnt orange, camel, mustard, cream and teal objects, never neon. Not a filter: no vignette, no scratches, no VHS lines, no light leaks, no sepia, no faded Instagram look.

Natural motivated light only: low sun through windows, overcast daylight, practical lamps, fluorescent office tubes, the warm glow of a diner. Real lived-in places with everyday wear: scuffed floors, worn upholstery, a little clutter. Every object, car, sign, appliance, hairstyle and garment belongs to the stated year and place, with a believable mix of older and newer things. Nothing from a later year appears anywhere in the frame. Signs, labels and papers carry no readable brand names or invented words; keep text in the background out of focus.

Camera: steady, unhurried, eye-level. A locked or gently moving camera, never handheld shake, never rapid zooms, whip pans, slow motion or time-lapse. Let the surroundings show around the people. The shot stays the same size for its whole length unless a slow move is stated.

Performance: restrained and human. Small natural movements, real listening, no exaggerated gestures, no constant nodding, no frozen smiles, no unnatural blinking. Only the named speaker moves their mouth; the listener reacts with the eyes and stillness, lips closed.

Faces: every person is an original, ordinary-looking-but-attractive individual with the specific features described, natural skin texture and pores, natural asymmetry. Nobody resembles any real actor, celebrity or public figure. Background extras are varied in age, build and ethnicity, dressed for the period and the weather.

NO TEXT ON SCREEN: no titles, captions, subtitles, logos or watermarks; titles and subtitles are added afterwards.
LEFT and RIGHT in a face description mean the person's own left and right (the viewer's opposite side when they face the camera). Every person looks exactly the age given, no older.
