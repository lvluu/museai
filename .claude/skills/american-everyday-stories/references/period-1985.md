# Period check: small-city Pennsylvania, mid-1980s

## Everyday life, 1985
- **Money**: cash and personal checks everywhere; credit cards (Visa, MasterCard) common for big
  purchases, rare at a grocery till; ATMs exist at banks ("the MAC machine" in PA); debit cards at
  the till are not normal yet. Coupons clipped from the Sunday paper. Gas about $1.10-1.20 a gallon
  (verify for the month); full-service attendants still common; mechanical rolling-digit pumps.
- **Phones**: wall and desk phones, many still rotary, push-button growing; long coiled cords;
  answering machines with micro-cassettes in some homes; pay phones on corners, in diners, motels;
  long-distance calls cost by the minute and are kept short, cheaper after 11 pm and on weekends.
  No cell phones in ordinary hands (car phones exist only for the rich), no pagers for ordinary
  workers.
- **Office**: electric typewriters (correction ribbon), carbon paper, photocopier, telex or fax
  rare and new in a small branch office (verify per office), rolodex, inter-office envelopes with
  string closures, a single personal computer with a green or amber text monitor and floppy disks,
  dot-matrix printer with tractor-feed paper. Smoking at desks is still allowed in many offices.
  Women in skirt suits with padded shoulders, men in suits or sport coats with ties.
- **Home**: console or tabletop colour TV with a dial or early remote, rabbit ears or cable box;
  VCR in many but not all homes (rented tapes from a video store); countertop microwave; stereo
  with a turntable and cassette deck; Walkman for teenagers; harvest gold/avocado appliances from
  the 1970s next to newer almond ones.
- **Cars on the street**: mostly late-1970s to 1985 American sedans and wagons, some Japanese
  compacts, older pickups; boxy shapes, chrome bumpers on older cars, sealed-beam headlights. No
  modern aerodynamic shapes, no LED lights, no SUVs as family cars (a few Jeeps and Broncos).
  Seat belts often not worn (PA belt law came later; verify) and children ride in the back.
- **Clothes and hair**: feathered or permed hair, big soft waves; mustaches common on men;
  high-waisted jeans, crew and turtleneck sweaters, corduroy, down vests, nylon windbreakers,
  canvas sneakers and white leather sneakers. Not every person is "80s fashion": older people
  dress like the 1960s-70s.
- **Kids**: walk to school or ride the yellow bus; lunch boxes; Halloween costumes from a box
  with a plastic mask or homemade; Saturday-morning cartoons.
- **Speech**: "pop" not "soda" in western PA, "soda" in the east (central PA mixed); "you guys",
  "jeez", "no way", "gimme a break", "that's the pits", "take a hike". Avoid: "awesome sauce",
  "my bad", "no worries", "reach out", "circle back", "literally" as an intensifier every line,
  "dude" from adults, anything internet.
- **Travel**: paper road maps, the Pennsylvania Turnpike tickets and toll booths, motels with
  room keys on plastic tags, a TV bolted to the dresser, an ice machine.
