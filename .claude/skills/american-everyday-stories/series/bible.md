# Series bible: The Larkmont Years

Default series of the American Everyday Stories skill. A user who asks for another setting gets a
new bible made the same way (`series/<slug>/bible.md`, `cast.json`, `ledger.json`); this one stays.

## Premise
Autumn 1985. Nora Kessler, 23, comes home from Philadelphia to the small Pennsylvania city she
left at eighteen, into her widowed mother's house with her brother Tommy, 19, and kid sister Lucy, 9.
Their mother Joan works night shifts as a nurse at Larkmont General and is mostly heard, not seen.
New job, old neighbours, young people in a town that is changing slower than they are. Each episode is one ordinary
day-sized problem: a car that will not start, a lunch that goes wrong, a favour, a misunderstanding.

All people, businesses and the town are fictional. Never present an episode as a true story.

## World
- **Larkmont, Pennsylvania** (fictional): about 24,000 people on a river in the ridge-and-valley
  country of central Pennsylvania. Brick downtown from the 1910s-20s, a closed rail-car works by the
  river, frame and brick twin houses on hillside streets, a 1970s strip on Route 11 with a
  supermarket, a discount store and the first fast-food places. Wooded ridges on both sides turn
  orange and rust in October; first snow usually late November.
- Regional texture (generic, no real brands on screen): church suppers and fire-hall bingo,
  hoagies, scrapple and shoofly pie at the diner, high-school football on Friday night, deer
  season talk in November, people who say "redd up" and "slippy".
- Time runs forward. Episode 1 = Monday 14 October 1985. Every episode states its exact date.

Danny and Tommy used to fix cars together in the Kessler driveway. Grace argued for hiring Nora over
a man from Harrisburg and has not told her. Lucy decides Danny is her friend before Nora decides anything.
Danny and Nora: something unspoken from high school, never named on screen.

## Visual identity
The look of the reference film (see `references/look.md`): warm photochemical 1980s colour, golden
low sun, cream and amber highlights, slightly lifted blacks, rust/orange/brown/teal production
design, fine film grain, soft halation. Wide establishing views of streets and hills between scenes;
dialogue in stable two-shots and medium shots; interiors lit by windows and practical lamps.
