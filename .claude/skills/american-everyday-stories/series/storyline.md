# Storyline: The Larkmont Years, Season 1 ("Coming Home", autumn 1985)

The arc that runs under the stand-alone episodes in `bible.md`. Each episode is still one small,
complete day-sized story. This file says what each one moves forward, so episode N+1 can be written
from the ledger and this page alone. Episode 1 is the pilot.

## Season question
Did Nora come home because she failed, or because she chose to? She starts the season sure it was
failure and hiding it behind jokes. By the first snow she can say out loud that she chose
Larkmont, and she means it.

## Character arcs (season 1)
| who | starts | wants | learns / ends |
|---|---|---|---|
| NORA | Proud, embarrassed to be back, says yes to everything, never asks for help | Prove she is doing fine | Asking for help is not losing. In Ep 10 she is the one who helps Danny. |
| DANNY | Easy, cheerful, works at his uncle's station, takes night classes he never mentions | His own garage one day | Says the plan out loud to Nora in Ep 8; the season ends with him pricing a lease on Route 11 |
| GRACE | Exact, cool, protective of the job she argued Nora into | Law school (a letter she keeps in her drawer) | Lets Nora see she once made mistakes too (Ep 5); tells Nora about the letter in Ep 9 |
| TOMMY | Charming, unfinished, fixes everything except his own plans | His band to play somewhere real | Keeps his promises (Corolla, TV, costume, amp). In Ep 7 he talks about their father for the first time. |
| LUCY | New kid at school, blurts the truth | A friend | Finds Tasha in Ep 4. She is the one who says what the grown-ups won't. |
| ELLIE | Knows everything about everyone | To be told things first | Becomes Nora's ally in public (Ep 2, Ep 8) |
| JOAN (heard, not seen) | Night shifts at Larkmont General | Her kids to be all right | Seen for the first time at Thanksgiving (Ep 9) |

Danny and Nora: something unspoken from high school. It stays unnamed all season. It moves only
through favours, small honesty and who drives whom.

## How to use this file
- Before writing an episode: read the ledger, then this table row. Bring forward at least one planted
  thread and plant at least one new one. Put both in the episode's `continuity_update`.
- The film shows the series frame. The opening card reads "THE LARKMONT YEARS · EPISODE N" with the
  episode title. An end card teases the next title (`title.json` `series`, `episode`, `next`).
- When the user changes an arc, edit this file and log the change in `ledger.json` `corrections`.
