HEALING POV RITUAL FOOTAGE REQUIREMENTS (reference 7: "POV : All you need is flowers and a cup of tea")

NO TEXT ON SCREEN. Real phone footage of one small home ritual in a real village house: warm low sunlight (morning or late afternoon), rich but natural colours (rust, honey stone, deep green leaves, clear blue sky outside), bright cream rooms with warm dark wood, rattan and plain linen indoors, fine sensor grain. Real plants and food for this region and season only. It must look filmed, not generated: no orange HDR glow, no halos, no rim light, no candles or lamps, nothing in perfect rows, food handmade and uneven.

CAMERA: held almost still like a phone in the hand, tiny natural sway, level horizon, no push-in, no pan, no zoom, no camera or tripod in frame.

LIFE IN THE FRAME (real time, never a time-lapse): ONE small action per shot, finished calmly - a step on the path, a hand opening a door, lifting a jug, pulling a drawer, pouring, an animal walking at natural speed, stems moving in a breeze. Buildings, furniture, china and jars stay rigid and sharp; nothing warps, melts or morphs; objects do not appear, vanish or rearrange themselves.

THE GIRL (only when the shot mentions her; the SAME woman as every healing video): about 26, long chestnut-brown hair in a loose low braid, in exactly the clothes the shot names. Slender young hands, smooth pale-peach skin, short clean rounded nails with natural pale-pink polish, exactly five fingers. Seen from behind, from the side, from the waist down or as hands; her face is NEVER shown and she never looks into the camera or speaks.

ANIMALS (only when named): one real animal of normal size and anatomy, calm, natural speed, never doubled, never cartoonish.

SOUND: the real close-miked sound of the shot's action (boots on gravel, a cupboard door, china, tea pouring, a duck's soft quack) over birdsong or quiet room tone. NO music, no voice, no speech.

Natural lived-in realism, not a cartoon, painting, advertisement or game render. No watermarks, no subtitles, no logos.
