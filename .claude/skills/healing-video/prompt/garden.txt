HEALING GARDEN MONTAGE FOOTAGE REQUIREMENTS (reference 5: "If a boring life looks like this")

NO TEXT ON SCREEN. Real phone footage of an ordinary country garden life on a sunny day: bright, vivid but natural colours (deep leaf greens, a clear blue sky with white clouds, red brick or warm stone, ripe fruit), fine sensor grain, real wear. Real vegetation and flowers for this region and season only. It must look filmed, not generated: no orange HDR glow, no halos, no rim light, no candles or lamps, no arranged-for-a-photo props, nothing in perfect rows; handmade food is uneven.

CAMERA: held still like a phone resting in the hand, tiny natural sway at most, level horizon, no push-in, no pan, no zoom, no camera or tripod in frame. Outdoors, in the open air, in real sunshine with soft clear shadows.

LIFE IN THE FRAME (real time, never a time-lapse): one gentle motion per shot - leaves and grass in a light wind, a cat or dog or hen moving at natural speed, a hand placing one piece of food, a bird crossing the sky. Buildings, windows, baskets, plates and fruit stay rigid and sharp; nothing warps, melts or morphs; objects do not appear, vanish or rearrange themselves.

THE GIRL (only when the shot mentions her; the SAME woman as every healing video): about 26, long chestnut-brown hair in a loose low braid, in the dress the shot names. Slender young hands, smooth pale-peach skin, short clean rounded nails with natural pale-pink polish, exactly five fingers. Seen from behind, from the side or as hands, never looking into the camera, never speaking.

ANIMALS (only when named): one real animal of normal size and anatomy, relaxed, natural speed, never doubled, never cartoonish. Animals in bright daylight only.

SOUND: the real close-miked sound of what happens in the shot (a stem softly rustling, a cat's soft meow, a hen's cluck, leaves rustling) over birdsong and a light breeze. NO music, no voice, no speech.

Natural lived-in realism, not a cartoon, painting, advertisement or game render. No watermarks, no subtitles, no logos.
