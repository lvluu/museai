# Healing account kit (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, Threads), 2026-11

One account per platform for healing / slow-living videos only (user rule 2026-11-02). Kitchen and home
clips on the Travel account lose travel viewers, and one account that mixes formats confuses the
platforms' interest matching (social-captions/references/posting-times.md, "Channel mix"). You create
every account by hand; the agent never signs in or creates accounts. The videos are AI-made and the
account says so openly (see "AI disclosure").

## Name and handle
Use the same handle on all six platforms so the account is easy to find. X allows 15 characters at most,
so every option below fits. Check each platform yourself before you pick: on 2026-11-02 only YouTube
could be checked read-only (`youtube.com/@<handle>` returned 404 = free). TikTok showed a bot check, and
Instagram, Threads and X need a sign-in to show a profile, so they were not checked.

|---|---|---|---|
| **Slow Sunlit Days** (recommended) | `@slowsunlitdays` | free | Says the look in three words: every video is a bright, sunny, unhurried day (SKILL.md "Real look"). "Slow" matches the slow-living search term. Not tied to a country or a season. |
| Slow Honey Days | `@slowhoneydays` | free | Warm, soft, cottage feel; "honey" also reads as golden-hour light. |

| Gently Home | `@gentlyhome` | free | Shortest; "gently" echoes the "living gently" captions. |

Taken on YouTube (don't use): `slowlittledays`, `gentlehours`, `softlyhome`, `quietkettle`,
`linenandlight`, `slowkettle`, `lazysundays`, `sunnyslowlife`.

If `@slowsunlitdays` is taken somewhere, take the next option on all six platforms rather than a
variant (`slowsunlitdays_`) on one: one handle everywhere matters more than the first-choice name.

Display name (searchable, shown above the bio): `Slow Sunlit Days · slow living`.
Instagram allows 30 characters in the name field, and "slow living" in it makes the profile show up when
people search that term.

## Niche settings (warm-up signal)
Platforms decide who sees a new account partly from what the account itself watches and follows. Before
the first post, teach each one the niche:

- **Follow about 20 niche creators** per platform: slow-living, cottage kitchen, home baking, herb
  garden, countryside life and calm-vlog accounts (search the topic tags below and pick accounts that
  post often and get comments). No travel, no chinese-lesson, no dream-life accounts.
- **Watch niche videos to the end**, like a few, save one or two, for 10-15 minutes a day.
- **Search the keywords** a few times: `slow living`, `cottagecore`, `healing video`, `cozy home`,
  `countryside life`, `baking bread`, `herb garden`.
- **Topic tags** (3-5 per post, never `#fyp`, `#viral`, `#foryou`): `#slowliving #cottagecore #healing
  #cozyhome #countrysidelife`, swapping one for the video's subject (`#homebaking`, `#herbgarden`,
  `#catsofinstagram`). Instagram caps posts at 5 hashtags.

## AI disclosure
The healing videos are photoreal (the girl, the homes, the animals), so every platform's rule applies:
- **Instagram / Facebook:** *Add AI label* on every reel (Advanced settings); the AI-generated profile
  label on Instagram (since 2026-08-31 unlabelled AI-person profiles can stop being recommended).
- **YouTube:** *altered or synthetic content* = Yes (museai sets `containsSyntheticMedia` when it uploads).
  YouTube does not monetize mass-produced, repetitive videos: every healing video has its own idea, home
  and music (the idea bank and history make sure), so never re-upload one with small changes.
- **TikTok:** the AI-generated content toggle (museai sets `is_aigc`).
- **X / Threads:** say "AI-made" in the bio; that is the disclosure.

## Monetization prerequisites
Check each in the platform's own monetization page once the account grows; numbers as of 2026-11.
- **YouTube Partner Program:** 1,000 subscribers + 10 M Shorts views in 90 days (or 4,000 watch hours);
  the lower tier (fan funding) opens at 500 subscribers, 3 uploads in 90 days and 3 M Shorts views.
  Needs 2-step verification and an AdSense account.
- **TikTok Creator Rewards:** 18+, 10,000 followers, 100,000 views in 30 days, and only videos longer
  than 1 minute earn. The 10-30 s healing videos do not; a `--shot 3` long cut over 60 s would.
- **Facebook:** Content monetization is by invitation (Professional dashboard → Monetization); a Page
  must follow Partner Monetization Policies, and original, labelled videos qualify.
- **Instagram:** Gifts and subscriptions open from the Professional dashboard once eligible; no per-view
  pay for Reels.
- **X:** Creator revenue sharing needs Premium, 500 verified followers and 5 M impressions in 3 months.
- **Threads:** no direct pay; it brings followers to Instagram.

## Cross-linking
- **Threads from Instagram:** sign up to Threads with the Instagram account (same handle and picture).
- **Facebook Page ↔ Instagram:** link them in Accounts Center. Instagram's *Share to Facebook* then works,
  but auto-post uploads the original file to each platform separately, which is better (no watermark).
- **YouTube / TikTok / X:** put the YouTube channel link in the bio and the Instagram handle in the
  YouTube description's last line.

## Posting plan
**One video a day, at the US evening** (the dashboard's **Healing** channel: `posting.py`
`CHANNELS["healing"]`, one slot). The times are `TIMES["healing"]` (slot 1 of the dream table): the hour
whose first two hours fall in the evening peak of most US viewers, weighted by time zone.

New York time (→ GMT+7, next morning):

| | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube Shorts | 20:00 | 20:00 | 21:00 | 21:00 | 21:00 | 21:00 | 21:00 |
| TikTok | 20:45 | 20:45 | 20:45 | 20:45 | 20:45 | 20:45 | 20:45 |
| Facebook | 20:00 | 20:00 | 20:00 | 20:00 | 21:00 | 20:00 | 21:00 |
| Instagram | 21:00 | 21:00 | 21:00 | 20:00 | 21:00 | 21:00 | 21:00 |
| X / Threads | 21:00 | 21:00 | 21:00 | 21:00 | 21:00 | 21:00 | 21:00 |

New York 21:00 = 09:00 next day in Vietnam (08:00 from mid-March, US summer time); the dashboard
converts it. Never two videos a day: a second one competes with the first's seed-audience test.
Captions come from `../social-captions` (`captions.py check` must PASS), opening with the video's line
("the way I want to live:") and ending with one question (which home, which season, which corner).

## Connect the accounts to the Healing tab (dashboard)
Auto-post covers **YouTube, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram** (`services/publishers` `AUTO_PLATFORMS`);
X and Threads stay manual from the Ready-to-post card. Accounts are stored per channel
(`data/oauth.json`), so connecting the Healing row never touches the Travel or Dream accounts. The
developer apps from docs/auto-post.md are shared; nothing new to register.

1. `/dashboard` → **Auto-post → Connected accounts** → the **Healing** row.
2. **YouTube → Connect:** in Google's chooser, pick the **Slow Sunlit Days** brand channel (not your
   personal or Travel channel). The row then shows the channel name; check it.
3. **Facebook / Instagram → Connect** (one Meta login gives both): tick the new Page and its linked
   Instagram in Meta's dialog, then pick them in the dashboard if the login manages several Pages. The
   Instagram account must be Creator and linked to the Page first, or it does not appear.
4. **TikTok → Connect:** sign in to the new TikTok account in the browser first (TikTok connects the
   account that is signed in). Needs the tunnel and `MUSE2API_PUBLIC_BASE` only while you click
   Connect (docs/auto-post.md, TikTok step 4). Until the app's audit passes, TikTok posts stay private.
5. **Rules → Add rule:** channel = `Healing`, pattern = `healing-*` (there is no healing *source* in
   `autopost.py` `SOURCES` yet, so match by pattern), platforms = YouTube first, schedule = slot,
   thumbnail = 9x16, then **Save rules**. Tick Instagram, Facebook and TikTok once the first YouTube
   post shows `posted`.
6. X and Threads: post by hand from the card at the times above, with the card's X / Threads caption.

A job that comes due without an account waits in `needs_account` and turns `missed` after 2 h: connect
before the first video lands on the Healing tab.

## What not to do
- No `#fyp`, `#viral`, `#foryou`, no more than 5 hashtags.
- Don't post the TikTok download elsewhere (watermark loses reach on Instagram and Facebook).
- Don't buy followers or use follow/unfollow tools: the first audience decides who sees the account next.
- Don't post travel, lessons or dream videos here, and don't post healing videos on the Travel account.
- Don't post the same video twice.
