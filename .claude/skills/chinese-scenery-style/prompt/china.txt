CHINESE SCENERY STYLE

REAL FOOTAGE: it must look like real documentary footage filmed on a cinema camera with a 35 mm lens, never CGI, a render or AI art. People look like real people: clear, even, healthy young skin with a natural fine texture (no freckles, no spots, no moles, no blemishes, no sun-damage marks), natural asymmetry, real hair strands, real proportions, real cloth weave and fur, natural relaxed movement. No beauty filter, no airbrushed or plastic skin, no doll-like face, no oversized eyes, no glossy sheen.

Cinematic, photorealistic travel footage of a real place in China, vertical 9:16, shot on a full-frame cinema camera. The scenery has generous screen space: mostly wide and medium-wide compositions, the traveller small to medium in frame, never a face close-up.

REAL PLACE: the landmarks, architecture, terrain, water and vegetation named above, exactly as they really are. Chinese and Tibetan architecture keeps its real proportions, roof shapes, colours and carvings; no invented temples, pagodas, bridges, mountains or lakes, no duplicated buildings. Signs and banners stay blurred or turned away: no readable text anywhere. Religious places are filmed respectfully from visitor paths: no staged rituals, no monks or worshippers filmed up close.

ONE SEASON, ONE DAY: {SEASON}. {LIGHT}.

LOOK: the clear, bright Chinese travel-film look (通透, 清新): crisp transparent mountain air, rich but natural colours, a clean, soft clear-blue sky (never an inky saturated blue), golden grass a soft natural gold rather than orange, warm sunlight making the reds, golds and whites glow, fresh greens and golden grass, bright well-exposed faces and shadows that keep detail. Never hazy, washed-out, grey, dark, moody or heavy. No oversaturation, no orange-teal grading, no HDR halos, no fantasy colours.

CAMERA: {MOVE} Almost still, like a photograph that breathes: a tripod shot, or one restrained move at one constant very slow speed for the whole clip, the frame drifting under a twentieth of its width per second so every detail can be seen clearly; calm air, grass, flags and water only stir gently in a light breeze, never whipping in the wind; level horizon, already moving in the first frame. Every frame tack-sharp, no motion blur: never pan or glide sideways across close foreground (grass, flowers, carvings smear); scenery is locked off or a slow straight push-in, pull-back or rise. No handheld wobble, no shake, no bounce, no orbiting, no whip, no abrupt zoom, no drone dive, no sudden reframing, no speed change.

MOTION: real-time footage, never a time-lapse and never a frozen photo: grass, water, prayer flags, steam and people move at their true speed, clouds stay where they are. Buildings, mountains and streets stay perfectly rigid.

SOUND: natural location sound only ({AMBIENCE}), clean and quiet; no music, no narration, no dialogue, no singing.

SCREEN: no subtitles, captions, logos, watermarks or text of any kind.
