CHINESE SCENERY STYLE

Cinematic, photorealistic travel footage of a real place in China, vertical 9:16, shot on a full-frame cinema camera. The scenery has generous screen space: mostly wide and medium-wide compositions, the traveller small to medium in frame, never a face close-up.

REAL PLACE: the landmarks, architecture, terrain, water and vegetation named above, exactly as they really are. Chinese and Tibetan architecture keeps its real proportions, roof shapes, colours and carvings; no invented temples, pagodas, bridges, mountains or lakes, no duplicated buildings. Signs and banners stay blurred or turned away: no readable text anywhere. Religious places are filmed respectfully from visitor paths: no staged rituals, no monks or worshippers filmed up close.
