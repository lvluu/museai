# healing-story

Paths are relative to this folder (`.claude/skills/healing-story/`). Server, render queue, voice, music, compose, QA and delivery come from `../healing-video/SKILL.md` (Story mode) and `../muse-video/SKILL.md`; this skill changes what the story says and what the shots show. Name everything `healing-<slug>`; the work folder is `~/Movies/museai/healing-<slug>/`.

## The format (user 2026-11-12)
The user found Story mode videos that narrate the recipe less meaningful: "don't need to say anything related to cooking, just show cooking actions, but tell the real story".
- **The voice tells a life story, not the recipe.** First person, one real-feeling story with one turn and one lesson (a stranger who became a best friend, a parent's small habit, a letter never sent). At most two lines mention the food, and only as part of the story ("She's the reason I'm baking tonight", "Gingerbread, a little burnt, the way she likes it").
- **The picture shows calm cooking and home actions that RHYME with each line** (the line and the shot mean the same thing without saying it):
  | line | shot |
  |---|---|
  | alone in a new city | one egg cracked into a big empty bowl |
  | "she just kept showing up" | two mugs set down side by side |
  | she moved away | hands taping a moving box |
  | the friendship grows | two pairs of hands kneading one dough |
  | the first knock (hook) | the friend at the door, face visible, a plate in her hands, smiling as it opens; the last beats: she stands at a new door (bookend) |
  Memories are cutaways in the same home (a stairwell, a window, a doorway); the present day is her baking for the person in the story, so the cooking leads to the ending (the friend arrives, two mugs).
- **Story arc** (~35 beats, 400-600 words, 3-4 min): hook (a confession or promise, the first sentence) → present-day frame in one line ("she lands in two hours, so let me tell you how we met") → the lonely before → the small moment that changed it → the friendship in three quick images → a quiet loss or distance → the present day → the lesson as an action the viewer can take ("If you know someone who's alone this Christmas, knock") → a two-word close ("Merry Christmas.").
- **English**: natural, casual, native spoken English: short sentences, contractions, everyday words, a little humour ("a fridge with one jar of mustard"), 1-2 sentences per beat. Dialogue goes into the voice-over as reported speech ("And she said, hey, are you doing anything tonight?"). No poetic words.
- **Restrained emotion**: the voice may say "I cried"; the picture never shows it. Sad beats are calm shots from behind, hands still, eyes on the thing. Big moments (meeting, reunion) are hands or two people side by side from behind.

## Shots
`scripts/shot "<brief>" sN.mp4` appends `prompt/$SEASON.txt` (default `christmas`): the plain real look, the girl's fixed look (same as healing-video), the friend's fixed look, faces shown naturally, one light source, close-miked sound. It refuses bait words (cosy, glow, candle, fairy lights, bokeh, glitter, crying, tears, knife, knuckles, door handle; `BRIEF_OK=1` overrides) and renders 16:9 at 6 s.
- One shot per beat; each brief names who (the girl / Mia / both / hands), what the hands do, the light ("bright soft daylight from a snowy window", or "Evening." first for dusk shots), and the sound of the action.
- **Same faces in every shot (user 2026-11-13, third cut: "Mia's face is not consistent across all scenes")**: text alone gives a new face per shot. Every shot with a face starts from a still made with the character bank (`../character-bank`, cards `MIA` and `ELLIE`, the girl): `python3 ../character-bank/scripts/bank.py scene v3/ "<the first moment of the shot>" MIA ELLIE --size 1920x1080 --n 1 --look prompt/christmas.txt`, then the clip from that still: `IMAGE=<still>.png scripts/shot "<the action>" sN.mp4`. Shots of hands or of a person from behind stay text-only.
- **Clothes change with time (user 2026-11-13, fourth cut: "change clothes for the main character, not always the same clothes since time is different")**: before rendering, write an outfit plan in `story.json` (`outfits: {"<time>": {"ELLIE": "...", "MIA": "..."}}`, one entry per day or year of the story: the first Christmas, the winter after, spring, tonight) and give each beat its `time`. Every still and shot brief names the outfit of its time ("Ellie, in a heather-grey sweatshirt, ..."). The same time keeps the same outfit across its shots; hands-only shots name the sleeves ("her hands in burgundy knit sleeves"). The prompt file never fixes clothes.
- **One face, checked by eye (fourth cut: "the face of Mia seems not consistent across the video")**: the bank still drifts too (longer hair, wider face, the mole gone). Render 2 takes per still and lay each next to `../character-bank/bank/<KEY>/hero.jpg`; keep a take only when hair length, parting, face shape and the mole all match, else render again. Every scene brief repeats the marks ("her short black bob ending at the jaw, the small mole under her left eye"). The clip drifts as the head turns: one small head movement per shot, faces at medium size, and check the clip's first, middle and last frames against the hero; a face that changes mid-clip is re-rendered.
- **Doors stay doors (fourth cut: "she knocked on the other girl's door, the door suddenly disappeared")**: a doorway shot keeps the door leaf and the frame in view for the whole shot, from the first frame. Knocking at a door: the knocker in the hallway in front of a closed door (a whole person, beat 1 and the bookend), then the next shot from the hallway: the door open, the other person just inside the frame, the door edge visible. Never a person standing "in the doorway" with no door, or a doorway that opens onto a room with no frame.
- **Scan the whole cut before delivery**: a sheet of every shot (`fps=0.5,scale=320:-1,tile=5x1` per clip, 6 clips per page) and look at each row for: a face that is not the bank face, wrong or unchanged clothes for that time, a door or object that vanishes, two people facing the camera, a frozen or exaggerated smile. Re-render those shots before compose.
- **Reactions take time (fifth cut: "the girl opens the door, suddenly her eyes look surprised but she takes the plate right away, not natural")**: one change of feeling per 6 s shot, never two. A stranger at the door is calm and a little unsure, listens, and only slowly softens; she does not take a gift in the same shot she first sees it. Accepting is its own shot: a glance down, a short hesitation, then both hands come up slowly. Never write a peak expression ("surprised", "shocked", "delighted") into a still: the clip starts there and has to jump to the next feeling at once. Write the calm starting face in the still and the slow change in the motion.
- **Natural people, not AI people (sixth cut: "awkward smiling and laughing together for no reason", "the wrong-door scene shown three times", "no Christmas decoration at home", "their voices too loud")**:
  - Every still starts on a calm, relaxed face, mouth closed. Never write "smiling", "laughing" or "grinning" into a still brief: muse turns it into a wide grin and the clip opens mid-laugh. A smile appears in the motion only, small, and only after something in the shot causes it (a line said, a flour smudge noticed, a gift looked at).
  - Never two people laughing together. Warmth between people = a glance, a small nod, a quiet smile, working side by side.
  - One event = one shot. The knock, the wrong door, the first meeting: shown ONCE, then the narrator tells the rest fast over cutaways (the soup bowl, the stairs), never the same doorway two-shot over 3-4 beats.
  - Doorway exchanges at ordinary pace: open, small polite smile, one short quiet line, look at the gift, take it with both hands.
  - Season at home: in a holiday story every home, kitchen and hallway shot carries the season's real decor (Christmas: a small tree with warm lights, a pine wreath or garland, snow at the window), plain and lived-in, never a decor showroom.
  - Clip voices stay under the narration: `story169.py` ducks the clips under the voice and limits peaks; a beat whose people talk gets `"foley_db": -15` in story.json.
- **Camera stays put (moms-first-cake, 2026-11-13: 9 of 38 clips drifted, panned or jumped to a new framing)**: muse follows a person who walks or "turns toward" a place, so every brief is one stationary action ("stands up from the bed and stays where she is; the camera stays locked off, it does not follow her"). Never a process verb (sponges "rising", "baking"): it renders as a time-lapse; write the finished state and "in real time nothing changes". Hands-only shots name the sleeves, a container keeps its name and its contents across shots ("the white enamel colander, which already holds the strawberries and raspberries"), and a knock is never shown (the knocker raps the air beside the door): start on the person already waiting.
- **Trim, don't re-render, a bad ending**: most drifts start after 3-4 s and the voice lines are 2-6 s. Keep the clean head (`ffmpeg -i full.mp4 -t 3.6 -c:v libx264 -crf 16 -c:a aac sN.mp4`, or `-ss` to skip a bad start) and keep the full take in `rej/`; story169 stretches it to the line (fine down to ~0.7x). Re-render only when the first 3 s fail. The server dedupes an unchanged brief (same video back): change the wording to get a new take.
- **Human behaviour, not AI batch action (third cut)**: "two people laughing look awkward, mid laughs also awkward", "action should be real like human behaviour". Each shot is one ordinary action at real-life speed with a small pause; one person acts and the other reacts a moment later; warm small smiles and a short chuckle at most, never both laughing open-mouthed. A first meeting is told in its real order: the friend knocks (whole person, beat 1), the girl walks to the door and opens it, the friend at the door says her line, the reaction.
- **People act like people (user 2026-11-12, second cut)**: "two girls should talk to each other and laugh freely, show Mia's face", "many scenes look awkward, not like human". Moments between people are shot like a film conversation (fourth cut): over the shoulder of the listener onto the speaker's face, the two looking at each other, never both facing the camera side by side; talking across a counter, one passing the other something. Name the feeling and the small natural gesture ("Mia says something short with a grin; Ellie smiles a moment later"). Nobody looks into the lens. A task alone stays "seen from the side, eyes on X". Never shoot a person only from behind for a whole shot when the line is about them.
- **Doors, knocks and sharp tools look fake in close-up** (that cut: a disembodied fist knocking, a hand pressing a handle, a door swinging by itself, a knife passed handle first). Show the whole person at the door in a medium shot (standing in the stairwell with the plate, the door already open), never a hand on a handle or a knocking fist. Never hand over or hold up a knife; give the hands something soft (a bowl of potatoes, an apron, a mug).
- Food keeps its colour in every shot (orange carrot soup, dark-edged gingerbread). The decor stays small: name "the small tree's warm-white string lights" or "a small simple wreath", never a list of decorations.
- **One cake, one family (moms-first-cake fifth cut, user 2026-11-14)**: "the cake is not consistent"; "she surprise bring the cake ... when she open the door"; "should have brother, dad"; "everyone sit around the table, sing happy birthday, talk and laugh naturally, no need their voice". A hero object that appears in many shots (the cake) gets ONE reference image: render it once, then pass it to every still that shows it with `bank.py scene --prop "the birthday cake=<work>/cakeref.jpg"` and animate the still (`IMAGE=`); text alone gives a new cake every shot. After it is blown out, every later still says "five plain white unlit candles with black wicks, no flames anywhere" or the flames come back. Everyone named in the story (Dad, brother) is in the family scenes, with a bank card each. Singing and table talk are seen, never heard: mouths move, the narrator's voice tells it (`foley_db: -15`). The closing grateful line is the narrator's own words to her mother, not the mother's.
- **Continuity bible (moms-first-cake sixth cut, user 2026-11-14)**: "the cake is cut, but when showing table with 4 people eating, the cake is still there"; "always 4 people on the table, not suddenly two"; "she shouldn't blow the cake"; "mom step downstairs and see surprise, not open the door and only the girl give the cake"; "the room should have decoration for birthday"; also "3 hands putting things in the basket", "putting cake into oven, there's nothing inside". Before any still, write per scene in `story.json` what is true there and keep it in every brief of that scene:
  - **Place references**: each place seen in 3+ shots (the decorated dining room, the stairs) gets one empty reference image, rendered once (`bank.py scene <dir> "<the room, nobody in it, every decoration named>"` with no cast) and passed to every still there with `--prop "the decorated dining room=<work>/roomref.jpg"`. The decor named in the reference is repeated in every brief of that place.
  - **Object states**: a hero object gets one reference per state (whole cake with lit candles; whole cake, candles out; cut cake, one slice missing, no candles), and every still uses the reference of the state at that moment of the story. Once a thing is cut, eaten, opened or filled, no later shot shows it as before.
  - **Who is there**: a scene with the whole family keeps all of them in every wide and medium shot ("all four always in frame": seat each one by name, same seat every shot). A close shot of one face still shows the others soft at the edge or behind. Nobody vanishes between two shots of the same scene.
  - **Who does what**: only the person the story gives the action does it (the mother blows the candles; the one carrying the cake never does; write "Nobody blows on the candles" in every shot before that). One hand per person, and in a hands-only shot "ONE hand only in the whole shot" or "two hands, never at the same time".
  - **Containers are full**: anything carried, put in or taken out names what is in it in the still and the shot ("a round tin filled almost to the top with smooth pale-yellow raw batter"); an empty tin into the oven is a re-render.
  - **A surprise is a family event**: the room is decorated, everyone in the story is waiting together (cake with lit candles held low at the waist), the honoured person comes to them (down the stairs, into the room), stops, slow reaction, then everyone says the line together. Never only one person at a door.
  - **No props that need precise hands**: no knife, no cake server in a hand, no cutting on camera. The slice is already on a plate; the hand only slides the plate.
- Hook shot (beat 1) starts mid-action with a sound you can see (a cutter in dough, a pour) or a person already in the moment (the friend smiling at the open door).

## Steps
1. **Story**: write `story.json` (`{"title", "season", "lesson", "beats": [{"vo", "shot"}]}`) in the work folder; keep a copy in `stories/<slug>.json` (the bank of told stories: never retell one; `ls stories/`). Show the user the title, hook and lesson; render after a yes unless they asked to just make it.
2. **Voice** (background, ~2 min): `uv run --no-project --python 3.11 --with "kokoro>=0.9" --with soundfile --with "misaki[en]" python ../place-story/scripts/vo.py story.json --speed 0.92` → `vo/bN.wav`.
3. **Shots**: one jobs line per beat with a retry, `../muse-video/scripts/render-queue -j 3 jobs.txt` in ONE background call:
   ```bash
   for t in 1 2 3 4; do scripts/shot '<brief>' ~/Movies/museai/healing-<slug>/sN.mp4; [ -f ~/Movies/museai/healing-<slug>/sN.mp4 ] && break; sleep 45; done
   ```
4. **Music**: `../healing-video/scripts/music "<season> piano lullaby, tender" p1.m4a <story seconds + 10> <seed>`; it must pass the calm gate (exit 3 → next seed). Christmas: "winter christmas piano lullaby, tender, warm"; no bells or jingles.
5. **QA**: contact sheet of all clips (`ffmpeg -i sN.mp4 -vf "fps=1,scale=320:-1,tile=6x1"`); re-render any shot with a face to camera, a stiff pose or frozen smile, a person who doesn't move like a person, crying, warped hands, a changed hair/sweater/scarf, wrong food colour, or decor clutter.
6. **Compose**: `python3 ../healing-video/scripts/story169.py story.json --music p1.m4a --out healing-<slug>.mp4` (small bottom subtitles, dissolves, ducked piano, -16 LUFS).
7. **Deliver**: 16:9 + 9:16 covers from a raw clip (never the composed video: subtitles burn in), title centred on the frame, style rotated from the cover history (`--style auto`, see healing-video step 7):
   ```bash
   python3 ../healing-video/scripts/cover.py --size 16:9 --at sN.mp4@auto --eyebrow "<season story>" --title "<hook>" --out thumbs/healing-<slug>_16x9.jpg
   python3 ../healing-video/scripts/cover.py --at sN.mp4@auto --eyebrow "<season story>" --title "<hook>" --out thumbs/healing-<slug>_9x16.jpg
   ```
   captions via `../social-captions` (no `#Shorts`), record with `../healing-video/scripts/ideas.py record custom ...`, then `FINAL=1 THUMB_DIR=thumbs CAPTIONS_FILE=captions.json ../muse-video/scripts/deliver.sh healing-<slug>.mp4 <work dir> healing-<slug>` (it trashes the work folder: record first, and copy `story.json`, the job scripts, reference images and still picks out of it before a FINAL run).

## Seasons
`prompt/<season>.txt` holds the full shot rules for one season and its cast. To add one (spring, Lunar New Year, autumn), copy `christmas.txt`, change the place, light, decor and sound lines, keep the girl's paragraph word for word, and run with `SEASON=<name>`. `mom.txt`: an English cottage kitchen and its fruit garden in early autumn, ELLIE and her mother ANNE (character bank card); everything on the cake is picked from the garden (story `stories/moms-first-cake.json`).
