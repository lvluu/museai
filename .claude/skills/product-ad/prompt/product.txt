PRODUCT FIDELITY (most important rule): The attached photo shows the real product being sold and is the exact first frame. The product stays exactly as in the photo for the whole video: every part keeps its colour, shape, size, order and count, and every letter, logo or printed mark on it stays sharp and identical. Letters never change, swap, blur into other letters or disappear. Nothing is added to the product or removed from it. The product is never redrawn, restyled, recoloured or replaced.

SCENE: Same props, surface, background and colours as the photo. No hands, no people, no new objects. No text, captions, stickers or graphics are added; any watermark already in the photo stays exactly where it is.

MOTION: REAL-TIME FOOTAGE, NOT A TIME-LAPSE AND NOT A STILL PHOTO. The product lies still on its surface. The only things that move: a soft, warm beam of light slowly sweeps across the product so glossy and transparent parts glint and sparkle as it passes, and the camera pushes in slowly and smoothly toward the product, level, no shake, no rotation. The product stays fully in frame and in sharp focus from the first frame to the last.

LOOK: Bright, clean, soft daylight, true colours as in the photo, crisp macro detail, shallow depth of field only on the background.
