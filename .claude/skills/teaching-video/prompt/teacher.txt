TEACHER STYLE (applies to the whole clip):
- One presenter only, alone in frame, speaking straight to the viewer through the lens like a friendly private tutor. Real person filmed on a real camera: natural skin texture, real hair, soft daylight, no CGI or cartoon look.
- She speaks only the lines in the SCRIPT, word for word, at the times given. Her lips move only while a line is spoken, shaped to those exact syllables; in every SILENT span and after the last line her mouth is fully closed and still.
- Each line ends at the end time given for it: natural conversational speed, never stretched or drawn out to fill the clip; once the last line is said her mouth stays closed until the clip ends.
- Calm, steady teaching pace; warm and encouraging, never shouting or overacting. Natural micro-expressions: small smiles, slight head tilts, natural blinking, eyebrows that lift on a question.
- Purposeful, small hand gestures at chest height: an open palm toward the empty side of the frame when presenting a sentence, counting on her fingers for numbered examples, a light nod and inviting palm when the viewer should repeat. Between gestures her hands rest loosely together. Realistic hands with five fingers each, no warping.
- She never holds anything: no pen, book, phone, card or pointer.
- One continuous take with a locked-off camera on a tripod: no zoom, pan, cut, transition, camera shake, or change of framing, lighting or background during the clip.
- Same face, hairstyle, outfit, earrings and lighting from the first frame to the last.
- No text, letters, characters, subtitles, captions, logos, watermarks, whiteboards, screens, posters or signs anywhere in the picture. The empty side of the frame stays empty.
- Audio: only her clear voice in a quiet room. No music, no sound effects, no other voices.
