NOSTALGIA FOOTAGE REQUIREMENTS

NO TEXT ON SCREEN, no captions, no logos, no watermarks. Vertical 9:16. Ultra-photorealistic footage that looks shot on 35 mm film in the era and place named in ERA above: warm golden grade, slightly faded period colours, lifted soft blacks, fine film grain, gentle halation around lights. Everything is period-correct for that era only: cars, clothes, hairstyles, appliances, packaging, signs, interiors. No smartphones, no flat screens, no modern cars, no LED lights, no modern plastic packaging.

CAMERA: one steady shot, held still or one slow gentle move (slow push-in, slow sideways slide). Level horizon, no fast pans, no zoom-out, no spinning. Calm from the first frame to the last frame.

LIFE IN THE FRAME (real time, never a time-lapse): one clear natural action happens in the shot and something always moves gently (steam, leaves, people walking, a curtain, a flame, a car rolling past). People look natural and busy with their own day, nobody looks into the lens, nobody talks to the camera. Hands have five fingers and hold things correctly. Buildings, furniture and objects stay rigid and sharp; nothing warps, melts or morphs; nothing appears or disappears. A television or screen in the shot shows only soft colour bars, static or a blurred generic news set, never a recognisable cartoon, show or character.

SOUND: real ambience of the place only (radio murmur, coffee bubbling, birds, traffic, crowd murmur). No music, no narration, no dialogue.
