AERIAL DRONE SHOT REQUIREMENTS

One continuous aerial drone shot of a real place, no cuts, no transitions, no text. {LIGHT}, vivid but natural colours: saturated green meadows and forests, turquoise or deep blue water, white and red-roofed buildings. High contrast, sharp detail, postcard look.

CAMERA: {PACE} The subject (the landmark named in the brief) stays large and exactly in the centre of the frame for the whole clip, with the horizon in the upper third. No shake, no wobble, no sudden turns, no zoom jumps, no fish-eye.

MOTION: real-time footage, not a time-lapse: clouds stay in place, light does not change. Buildings, mountains and shorelines stay perfectly rigid; only water, trees, boats, trains and cars move, at their real speed.

SOUND: none needed (the music is replaced in post).

SCREEN: no watermarks, no subtitles, no captions, no logos, no text of any kind.

Photorealistic drone footage, not a render, painting or cartoon.
