TRAVEL DOCUMENTARY REQUIREMENTS

Ultra-photorealistic 4K travel documentary footage of a real geographical destination. Authentic geography, realistic scale, natural color grading, physically accurate sunlight, believable shadows and atmospheric perspective. Accurate mountains, architecture, vegetation, water and weather specific to the actual location: only the vegetation that really grows there (no trees or green meadows on treeless Arctic, desert or high-alpine land).

Shot on a professional full-frame cinema camera with high-quality natural dynamic range. Smooth, slow, physically plausible camera movement that never makes a viewer dizzy (level horizon, no spinning, rolling, swinging or diving), natural depth of field, authentic environmental motion (wind in leaves, moving water, drifting clouds, people walking at normal speed).

No artificial saturation, no HDR halos, no fantasy landscapes, no invented landmarks, no distorted buildings, no repeated mountains, no unnatural waves, no fake-looking waterfalls, no flickering, no sudden transitions, no people appearing or disappearing. Prefer one continuous shot; when a cut is necessary, keep the same time of day, light direction, weather and colour grade across it.

NATURE MOTION

Real-time footage: nature visibly moves at its true real-world speed, never frozen and never a time-lapse. Water moves as real water: small wind ripples travel across the surface in one direction toward the shore, reflections break into moving streaks and re-form, the motion carries on through the whole clip. The picture must never wobble, warp, ripple or shimmer in place like heat haze or jelly; buildings, mountains and the shoreline stay perfectly rigid and sharp, only water, mist, clouds, smoke and foliage move. Real-time footage, not a time-lapse: the sky, clouds and mist look like 15 real seconds, so the same clouds stay in the same places from the first to the last frame and only drift a few pixels sideways; no new clouds form, no clouds grow, a mist band keeps its size, height and shape and only slides very slightly sideways, it never rises over the mountains; the light does not change. When a first-frame photograph is given, it is not a still image to animate with a warp effect: it is the first frame of real moving footage.

SOUND

Background instrumental music that suits the location's atmosphere, with clean professional mixing and subtle real-world environmental ambience. No spoken narration, no dialogue, no singing.

SCREEN

No watermarks, no subtitles, no captions, no logos or any other text on screen.

Natural documentary realism, not a cartoon, animation, painting, or video-game render.
