TRAVEL DOCUMENTARY REQUIREMENTS

Ultra-photorealistic 4K travel documentary footage of a real geographical destination. Authentic geography, realistic scale, natural color grading, physically accurate sunlight, believable shadows and atmospheric perspective. Accurate mountains, architecture, vegetation, water and weather specific to the actual location.

Shot on a professional full-frame cinema camera with high-quality natural dynamic range. Smooth, slow, physically plausible camera movement, natural depth of field, authentic environmental motion (wind in leaves, moving water, drifting clouds, people walking at normal speed).

No artificial saturation, no HDR halos, no fantasy landscapes, no invented landmarks, no distorted buildings, no repeated mountains, no unnatural waves, no fake-looking waterfalls, no flickering, no sudden transitions, no people appearing or disappearing. Prefer one continuous shot; when a cut is necessary, keep the same time of day, light direction, weather and colour grade across it.

SOUND

Background instrumental music that suits the location's atmosphere, with clean professional mixing and subtle real-world environmental ambience. No spoken narration, no dialogue, no singing.

SCREEN

No watermarks, no subtitles, no captions, no logos or any other text on screen.

Natural documentary realism, not a cartoon, animation, painting, or video-game render.
