Licence quick reference:
- **Public domain / CC0:** free to use, no attribution needed (but verify the status).
- **CC BY:** attribution required.
- **CC BY-SA:** attribution required, and derivatives share alike.
- **CC BY-NC:** ⚠ not suitable for a monetized channel.
- **CC BY-ND:** ⚠ no modifications (cropping and zooming can count).
- **Stock / music library:** check that the plan covers YouTube monetization.
- **AI tools:** check the plan allows commercial use of outputs (image generator, voice).
