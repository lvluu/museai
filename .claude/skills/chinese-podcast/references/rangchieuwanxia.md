## Recurring patterns
- **Idea**: one quote, usually a relationship truth (who stays, who leaves, family by heart not blood, the start of
  knowing someone) or a gentle push to live fully (the wide world, your own sky). Said as a calm fact, never a lecture.
- **Story shapes** beyond `f21 先说结论`:
  - question then definition: 什么是X？ → one-line answer → 不管… → 不是靠…而是靠… (`f22 什么是`)
  - vow to oneself, then two groups of people, then calm non-attachment (`f23 把自己养好`)
  - three "not yet"s, then the reason, then 不只是为了…更是为了… (`f24 还没去过的地方`)
  - one nature metaphor carries the truth: wildflower here, sunflower there (`f25 换个地方开花`)
  - correct an envy: the bright surface, the hidden cost (`f26 光鲜背后`)
  - a conditional wish: 如果…再一次…我希望… (`f27 如果再一次`)
- **Pictures**: two families. (a) *home vlog*: golden-hour kitchens, coffee, clay pots, flowers, fruit baskets, a
  garden, hands and backs only, cats (look `vlog-diary`). (b) *seasonal window*: one strong season colour (crimson
  maple, gold ginkgo, pink blossom, summer green) seen from a dark room or a garden path, a lone figure far away or in
  silhouette (look `season-window`). Plus the sunlit cottage / courtyard already in `sunlit-cottage`.
- **Pacing**: shots 3-6 s, locked or slow drift; cuts on caption changes; no title card except a rare handwritten
  white brush title (秋日咖啡馆).
- **Captions**: always three rows when present, but the order changes per reel (vi / pinyin / hanzi, or pinyin /
  hanzi / vi). The skill keeps its own fixed layout.
- **Voice**: the narrated reels share one low calm female voice (F0 161-172 Hz; already `xiaoxiao-dusk`); one older
  reel uses a brighter, quicker young female voice (F0 ~181 Hz, ~4 syllables/s) → new voice `xiaoyi-diary`.
- **Music**: a continuous bed that never drops out, steady loudness, no clear beat. Three moods: cosy acoustic home
  vlog (`kitchen-diary`), slow strings with a low drone for autumn windows (`ginkgo-strings`), bright piano and pad for
  summer light through leaves (`summer-komorebi`).
- **New quiet truths** (themes t53-t59): 亲人, 磁场, 世界, 光鲜, 刚认识, 绽放, 理所当然.
