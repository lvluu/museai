## What makes them feel deep
- **One idea per video**, said as a quiet truth, not a lesson: 得失皆是寻常, 有些人仅仅是遇见便已耗尽了所有运气.
- **Second person, gentle**: 你要明白…, 我们总以为…直到后来才懂… The turn ("直到后来才懂") is the heart.
- **Ordinary people, seen from behind or far away**: nobody performs for the camera; faces rarely shown. The viewer
  puts themselves in the frame.
- **Weather and time of day carry the mood**: rain, dusk, autumn leaves, morning light through trees.
- **Slow**: shots 2-4 s, slow push-in or locked camera, one gentle movement; no fast cuts.
- **Warm muted film grade**, soft contrast, slight grain, shallow depth of field.
- **Sound**: one narrator, slow and close-mic (radio-host closeness), long pauses at commas; music is a quiet
  piano / soft lo-fi bed under the voice, never louder than it.
- **Captions**: the line being spoken only, hanzi large, translation small above or below in a light box.
