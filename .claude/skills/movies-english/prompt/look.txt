LOOK, CAMERA AND PERFORMANCE: a short rom-com movie in the exact style of the memoriescore reels (references/reference-study.md)

Picture: a frame from a 1990s Hollywood romantic comedy shot on 35 mm Kodak film: warm golden light, rich clean saturated colour, real deep black shadows, soft rolled-off highlights that never clip, a gentle halation glow around lamps and windows, fine film grain, a slightly soft picture. Skin warm, golden and glowing with real texture, not matte and not plastic. Daylight scenes are sunny and golden (late afternoon sun, long shadows, deep blue sky or water); evening scenes are lit by warm amber lamps, candles, string lights or neon against cool blue night. Never flat, grey, milky or washed out, never teal-and-orange, never HDR, never razor-sharp, never a render, CGI or airbrushed skin. Faces in focus, the background softly out of focus.

Coverage like the memoriescore reels: every shot is short and says one thing. A line is a single of the speaker alone, a close-up or a medium shot, the face large in the upper half, eyes on the other person who stands just outside the frame beside the camera (the gaze lands just beside the lens, never into it, never away). Inserts of hands and objects (a dropped bag, a glass, a ring box, a note) carry the comedy. A wide shot shows the whole place and both people when something physical happens. Both people share the frame only in a wide or a two-shot, never a cramped over-the-shoulder angle. Something always moves inside the frame: a person walks in, kneels, drops, sips, turns, laughs; nobody stands still and talks.

Camera: a real film camera, eye level: a very slow gentle push-in or a slight natural handheld drift, never a fast move, never a whip, never a zoom. ONE CONTINUOUS TAKE, no cuts.

Acting: lively, open, real like young film stars of a 1990s rom-com: a wide grin with teeth, a gasp, a raised brow, a sly look, a laugh that reaches the eyes; big natural moves of the head, hands and body. Never stiff, never posed, never a frozen smile, never a glassy stare, never cartoonish. Natural blinks and breathing never stop.

Speech: natural American English at a normal conversational pace, the exact words given, nothing added. Only the speaker's lips move; the jaw opens on every syllable and the lips close when the line ends. Each person keeps one voice throughout. Sound: the voices and the quiet ambience of the place, no music.

Faces: 1990s supermodel beauty, very young (19-23): big voluminous blown-out hair, strong brows, glossy lips, period 1990s wardrobe and styling; the men boyish, tanned and handsome with thick hair. Nobody resembles any real actor, celebrity or public figure. Extras (a baker, a waiter, a kid, a dog, an old lady on the street) fill the world naturally and often carry the gag.

Vertical 9:16 frame: faces in the upper half; the area three quarters down stays calm because a tiny subtitle sits there. NO TEXT ON SCREEN: no titles, captions, subtitles, logos or watermarks. LEFT and RIGHT in a face description mean the person's own left and right.
