LOOK, CAMERA AND PERFORMANCE: a short rom-com movie in the exact style of the memoriescore reels (references/reference-study.md)

Picture: a frame from a 1990s Hollywood romantic comedy shot on 35 mm Kodak film: soft light, muted pastel colour like a faded Kodak print (pale beige, cream, grey, soft brown), the surroundings desaturated, deep blacks only in dark clothes and hair, soft rolled-off highlights that never clip, a gentle halation glow around lamps and windows, fine film grain, a slightly soft picture. Skin fair peach with a soft pink blush on the cheeks and deep glossy rose lips, muted like 35 mm film, soft and evenly lit, a smooth matte glow with fine real texture, never orange, golden-tan, yellow, oily or plastic; the lips are the only gloss, high-shine. Light exactly like the memoriescore reels: soft, frontal, low-contrast beauty light from one big diffused warm source beside the camera: the whole face evenly lit, no shadow side, no hard nose or cheek shadow, no hot spot; a gentle warm halo on the edge of the hair from a light behind. The background is pale, simple and desaturated (beige stone, cream plaster, grey, snow, a pale sky, soft brown wood), softly out of focus, no strong colour anywhere except the blush and the lips; any lamps or string lights bloom gently and never clip; a light haze as if shot through a diffusion filter, the frame slightly soft like a 1990s video still; a soft out-of-focus foreground piece (a shoulder, a glass, a door frame) sits at one side. The whole picture glows softly: the face is the brightest, flattest-lit thing in the frame, every lamp, candle and string light blooms a little, the surroundings stay muted. Never a busy or saturated background, never underexposed. Daylight scenes sit in soft overcast light or open shade (a pale sky, no hard sun, no golden hour); evening scenes are lit by warm lamps, candles or string lights, the faces staying peach, never amber. Never flat, grey, milky or washed out, never teal-and-orange, never HDR, never razor-sharp, never dramatic chiaroscuro, never a render, CGI or airbrushed skin. Faces in focus, the background softly out of focus.

Coverage like the memoriescore reels: every shot is short and says one thing. A line is a single of the speaker alone, a close-up or a medium shot, the face large in the upper half, eyes on the other person who stands just outside the frame beside the camera (the gaze lands just beside the lens, never into it, never away). Inserts of hands and objects (a dropped bag, a glass, a ring box, a note) carry the comedy. A wide shot shows the whole place and both people when something physical happens. Both people share the frame only in a wide or a two-shot, never a cramped over-the-shoulder angle. Something always moves inside the frame: a person walks in, kneels, drops, sips, turns, laughs; nobody stands still and talks.

Camera: a real film camera, eye level: a very slow gentle push-in or a slight natural handheld drift, never a fast move, never a whip, never a zoom. ONE CONTINUOUS TAKE, no cuts.

Acting: lively, open, real like young film stars of a 1990s rom-com: a wide grin with teeth, a gasp, a raised brow, a sly look, a laugh that reaches the eyes; big natural moves of the head, hands and body. The beats of the memoriescore reels: a slow look up and down, a double take, a head turn toward the camera, a lowered pair of sunglasses, a sip with the eyes still on the other person, a hair flip, a lip bite or a pout, a shrug, a hand to the chest, a slow smirk that grows; every reaction plays large and slow enough to read in two seconds, like a 1990s movie star. Never stiff, never posed, never a frozen smile, never a glassy stare, never cartoonish. Natural blinks and breathing never stop.

Speech: natural American English at a normal conversational pace, the exact words given, nothing added. Only the speaker's lips move; the jaw opens on every syllable and the lips close when the line ends. Each person keeps one voice throughout. Sound: the voices and the quiet ambience of the place, no music.

Faces: 1990s supermodel beauty, very young (19-23): big voluminous blown-out hair with a glow on its edge, strong brows, defined eyes, high-shine glossy lips that catch the light, gold jewellery (hoops, bangles), bold solid-colour period 1990s wardrobe (white, black, red, satin); the men boyish, tanned and handsome with thick hair. Nobody resembles any real actor, celebrity or public figure. Extras (a baker, a waiter, a kid, a dog, an old lady on the street) fill the world naturally and often carry the gag; an animal has real fur texture lit by the same warm key and reacts like a character (a lick, a yawn, a head tilt). Christmas: light snow falling, the tree or string lights the only light, their bloom allowed, deep blue or black behind the faces.

Vertical 9:16 frame: faces in the upper half; the area three quarters down stays calm because a tiny subtitle sits there. NO TEXT ON SCREEN: no titles, captions, subtitles, logos or watermarks. LEFT and RIGHT in a face description mean the person's own left and right.
