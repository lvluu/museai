LOOK, CAMERA AND PERFORMANCE: a short rom-com movie in the exact style of the memoriescore reels (references/reference-study.md)

Picture: a frame from a 1990s Hollywood romantic comedy shot on 35 mm film with an 85-105 mm portrait lens at f/2.8-4: warm softly diffused vintage glamour, NOT a faded vintage filter. Warm neutrals with restrained pink-red accents (honey beige #B49A7D, peach-rose skin #BD8978, rose lips #A6534E, taupe #8C8277, deep brown-black #170F0B); creamy whites, about 5,500 K daylight with a subtle warm finish; medium contrast with rich deep brown-black shadows in clothes and hair, never lifted or milky blacks; low-to-moderate saturation, the lips and cheeks a little richer. Skin medium-toned warm rose-tan, a full shade deeper than fair: a midtone that reads darker than the pale surroundings, red clearly stronger than yellow (rosy, never yellow, golden, orange, pale or porcelain), a soft rose flush on the cheeks, glossy muted rose lips; natural skin texture and pores under a light optical diffusion, never waxy, airbrushed or beauty-filtered; small controlled reflections only on the lips and gold jewellery. Light: a broad soft frontal key slightly to one side of the camera, gently modelling the face with soft shadows; a subtle warm backlight catching single hairs; smooth highlights that never clip, a restrained bloom on lamps. The face is clearly readable and the clear centre of the frame: big, evenly lit, eyes and lips crisp; smooth fine detail with no aggressive sharpening and no digital crispness. A CLEAN PICTURE: no film grain, no noise, no dust, no sparkle, no glitter, no specks, no flicker. Background: simple and calm, a few large soft shapes in muted warm grey-taupe, beige and cream, heavily and smoothly defocused into soft blobs, never a busy rack of small objects, never small repeating detail, no small bright points; a soft out-of-focus foreground piece (a shoulder, a glass, a door frame) may sit at one side. Every fabric smooth and solid at a glance (plain wool, satin, velvet, silk, leather, plush), no small busy texture or print: tiny detail crawls when people move. Daylight scenes in soft overcast light or open shade (no hard sun, no golden hour); evening scenes lit by warm lamps, candles or string lights, the faces staying rose-tan, never amber. Never teal-and-orange, never sepia, never HDR, never heavy fog or haze, never film scratches, never exaggerated bokeh, never dramatic chiaroscuro, never a render or CGI.

Coverage like the memoriescore reels: every shot is short and says one thing. A line is a single of the speaker alone in a tight close-up from the upper chest up, like the memoriescore reels: the face fills about half the frame width, the hair almost the whole width, the top of the hair about a tenth below the top edge, the eyes about 40 % down the frame, eyes on the other person who stands just outside the frame beside the camera (the gaze lands just beside the lens, never into it, never away). Inserts of hands and objects (a dropped bag, a glass, a ring box, a note) carry the comedy. A wide shot shows the whole place and both people when something physical happens. Both people share the frame only in a wide or a two-shot, never a cramped over-the-shoulder angle. Something always moves inside the frame: a person walks in, kneels, drops, sips, turns, laughs; nobody stands still and talks.

Camera: a real film camera, eye level: a very slow gentle push-in or a slight natural handheld drift, never a fast move, never a whip, never a zoom. ONE CONTINUOUS TAKE, no cuts.

Acting: lively, open, real like young film stars of a 1990s rom-com: a wide grin with teeth, a gasp, a raised brow, a sly look, a laugh that reaches the eyes; big natural moves of the head, hands and body. The beats of the memoriescore reels: a slow look up and down, a double take, a head turn toward the camera, a lowered pair of sunglasses, a sip with the eyes still on the other person, a hair flip, a lip bite or a pout, a shrug, a hand to the chest, a slow smirk that grows; every reaction plays large and slow enough to read in two seconds, like a 1990s movie star. Never stiff, never posed, never a frozen smile, never a glassy stare, never cartoonish. Natural blinks and breathing never stop.

Speech: natural American English at a normal conversational pace, the exact words given, nothing added. Only the speaker's lips move; the jaw opens on every syllable and the lips close when the line ends. Each person keeps one voice throughout. Sound: the voices and the quiet ambience of the place, no music.

Faces: 1990s supermodel beauty, very young (19-23): big voluminous blown-out hair with a glow on its edge, strong brows, defined eyes, high-shine glossy lips that catch the light, gold jewellery (hoops, bangles), bold solid-colour period 1990s wardrobe (white, black, red, satin); the men boyish, tanned and handsome with thick hair. Nobody resembles any real actor, celebrity or public figure. Extras (a baker, a waiter, a kid, a dog, an old lady on the street) fill the world naturally and often carry the gag; an animal has real fur texture lit by the same warm key and reacts like a character (a lick, a yawn, a head tilt). Christmas: light snow falling, the tree or string lights the only light, their bloom allowed, deep blue or black behind the faces.

Vertical 9:16 frame: faces in the upper half; the area three quarters down stays calm because a tiny subtitle sits there. NO TEXT ON SCREEN: no titles, captions, subtitles, logos or watermarks. LEFT and RIGHT in a face description mean the person's own left and right.
