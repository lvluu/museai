# Style catalogue: wardrobe, scenes, animals, beats, Christmas

Measured from 11 memoriescore.studio reels and 6 Christmas reels (2026-11-05, round 2). Pick from here
when writing a script or setting a reel's `setting`; the `wardrobe` keys live in `series/cast.json`.

## Wardrobe
Women: one bold solid colour per outfit, 1990s supermodel cut, always gold jewellery (hoops, a thin
chain, a bangle), big blown-out hair, high-shine glossy lips.
- Fabrics seen: satin, velvet, leather, faux fur, lace, sequins, boucle tweed, shearling, tartan wool.
- Cuts seen: strapless column, satin slip, halter, mini with a cropped cardigan, padded-shoulder skirt
  suit, white strapless with a straw hat and cat-eye sunglasses (Riviera), red one-piece swimsuit (pool),
  burgundy one-piece ski suit (snow), long camel wrap coat, fur coat over a sequin mini, opera gloves.
- Cast keys: VIV city/coat/night/cozy/snow/christmas/fur/riviera/suit; SIENNA snow/lodge/city/night/
  christmas/party/leather/velvet; MAYA office, home, night, beach, christmas, party, pool, suit.

Men: tanned, thick 90s hair, boyish.
- Seen: white shirt open at the chest with rolled sleeves, black suit over a black shirt, tuxedo with a
  loosened tie, long black overcoat with a plaid scarf, grey tee, camel overcoat, shirtless at the pool
  or at home, cream cable-knit turtleneck, shearling aviator jacket, forest-green wool overcoat with
  leather gloves, white blazer over a black turtleneck, ivory dinner jacket.
- Cast keys: JAMIE bakery/city/night/cozy/christmas/party/pool/winter; THEO snow/lodge/city/night/
  christmas/cozy/party/pool; LUCAS office, home, night, beach, christmas, party, pool, winter.

Props that read in one frame: dark sunglasses lowered on the nose, a straw hat, a wine or martini glass,
a red paper cup of cocoa, leather gloves, a stack of wrapped gifts, a small leather handbag, a bouquet,
an umbrella, a cigarette never.

## Scenes (each lit by one warm practical source, background darker than the face)
- Night restaurant: candles, dark wood panelling, city lights in the window, a waiter behind.
- Penthouse at night: one lamp, floor-to-ceiling window, the skyline black and gold.
- Cream kitchen in the morning: low sun from one window, dark cabinets, steam from a kettle.
- Garden party at dusk: string lights over a long table, dark hedges behind, glasses glowing.
- Autumn market / park fountain: low golden sun, brownstones with red foliage, dark water with coins.
- Rainy stairs or street at dusk: wet stone reflecting shop light, yellow cabs, one awning.
- Golden-hour suburb: a tree-lined street, long shadows, a golden retriever on a lead.
- Riviera boutique: dark interior, sun through the door, a white car outside.
- Lake Como pool / a lake dock in the rain: grey-green water, a weeping willow, wet skin.
- Grand entrance hall at Christmas, snowy brownstone street at blue hour, night carousel, decorated
  shop fronts at night, a dark living room lit only by tree lights, a dark party with bokeh lights
  (see Christmas below).

## Animals
Animals are a device, never set dressing: each gets one close-up insert (1-2 s, fur lit by the same
warm key, real fur texture) and one beat where a person holds, walks or talks to it.
- Golden retriever (adult on a lead in the suburb; a puppy carried in one arm): the "he is a catch"
  device, licks a chin, steals food, sits between the two people.
- Tiny white Pomeranian: the comedy device; tongue-out close-up, runs through snow, sits on her lap,
  is addressed by name as if it understands.
- Also usable in the same register: a tabby cat on a windowsill judging the scene, pigeons that scatter
  when she arrives, a carriage horse at the kerb, a parrot that repeats the last word.
- A kid in a knitted beret or a red jumper and an old lady in a plum coat are the human extras that
  carry gags the same way.

## Acting beats (held 2-3 s, large and slow enough to read)
Over-the-shoulder look back while walking away; sunglasses lowered with the eyes still on the other
person; a raised glass or cup without turning; hand on hip, chin up; eyes closed in exasperation or in
bliss (savouring snow, a smell); a hand to the face or chest; the jealous look down-and-up; a jaw drop;
a spit-take; a finger on the lips to the lens; a slow smirk that grows; a head bowed to hide a smile;
a hair flip; a slow hip-swinging walk; a double take; a gasp with both hands up; the puppy held up
to the camera.

## Christmas
Picture: light snow falling, one warm practical source (a tree, string lights, carousel bulbs,
candles, a fire), deep blue or black behind the faces, lights allowed to bloom. Palette: blue-black
night + amber light, red (tartan, ribbons, cups), cream and white (shearling, turtlenecks, snow),
gold (bells, ornaments, hoops), burgundy and forest-green coats. Christmas frames measure darker than
the restaurant reels: p25 15-45, p50 25-100, p75 55-130; only the lights reach white. `GRADE=film`
already lands there; do not brighten.

Settings (write them this way in `setting`):
- "a grand entrance hall at Christmas, gold-panelled double doors, a staircase wrapped in garland with
  red ribbons, a huge tree with warm white lights, stacks of wrapped gifts, marble floor, chandeliers
  and tree lights the only light"
- "a snowy brownstone street at blue-hour dusk, iron railings wrapped in warm string lights, snow piled
  on the steps, a park bench, a deep blue sky"
- "a vintage carousel at night in a Christmas market, white horses with gold poles, warm fairy lights
  behind, snow falling, black night sky"
- "decorated shop fronts on a snowy street at night, a gift-stacked window, a snow-covered tree with
  gold ornaments, giant gold bells with a red velvet ribbon"
- "a dark living room lit only by multicolour tree lights and a fire, a buffalo-plaid blanket, vintage
  ornaments"
- "a Christmas party in a dark apartment, a wall of string-light bokeh, candles, glasses glowing"
- "an alpine lodge living room at Christmas, a stone fireplace, a tree in warm lights, snow at the window"

Wardrobe keys: `christmas` for every character (tartan with a fur collar, shearling collars and cuffs,
cream turtlenecks, forest-green or black overcoats, leather gloves), `party` / `fur` for the dressed-up
version (fur over sequins, white blazer over a black turtleneck, ivory dinner jacket, velvet columns
with opera gloves), `cozy` / `lodge` indoors.

Beats: a gift stack carried in; the wrong-house double take; eyes closed savouring the snow; a sip of
cocoa with the eyes on her; an over-the-shoulder look past a shop window; the gasp with both hands up;
a laugh holding the carousel pole; a head bowed to hide a smile; a kid runs across; a puppy held up.

Plots that worked: wrong house → charm; exes meet again at the carousel ("Hi, again"); the handsome
stranger recognised a beat too late ("That was him"); the dog as go-between; the dog ate the
gingerbread. Ready scripts: `library/batch_xmas.json` (`ME-X001`..`ME-X004`).
