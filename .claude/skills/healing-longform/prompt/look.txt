HEALING LONG-FORM FOOTAGE REQUIREMENTS (16:9)

NO TEXT ON SCREEN. Real, unstaged phone-camera footage of one ordinary day in a small lived-in country cottage and its kitchen garden, like a calm slow-living vlog shot on an iPhone in landscape: clear natural exposure, fresh true colours (soft greens, sage, cream, pale wood, grey stone, muted rust), fine sensor grain, real wear and texture. The shot says the place, the hour and the weather; the place stays the same place in every shot (the story's place sheet is pasted into the brief).

REAL, NOT AI: it must look filmed, not generated. A plain, clean, tidy home with only a few things in each frame: the hands, the work, two or three objects. No clutter, no props stacked for a photo, no vase-candle-lamp still life. Garden produce is real and uneven: different sizes, a little soil, a nibbled leaf, never shop-perfect, never "a row of" anything. No orange glow, no HDR look, no halos, no rim light, no bokeh balls, no lens flares, no saturated teal-and-orange grade.

LIGHT: one real source per shot, bright enough to see everything: soft morning daylight, bright overcast, sun breaking through after rain, or soft blue dusk with one plain lamp on when the shot says dusk. Never murky, never dark, never golden-orange.

CAMERA: a static locked-off shot, completely still, level horizon, like a phone resting on a shelf. No camera, phone or filming gear anywhere in the frame. No push-in, no drift, no zoom, no pan, no tilt: every edge of the frame stays in the same place from the first frame to the last.

LIFE IN THE FRAME (real time, never a time-lapse): one gentle living motion per shot - the hands working slowly, steam, leaves in a light breeze, rain on glass, mist, a cat breathing or stretching, a robin hopping. Walls, furniture, doors, stone walls, tableware and window frames stay perfectly rigid and sharp; nothing warps, melts, shimmers or morphs. Objects stay where they are; nothing appears or disappears; a container keeps what is in it.

THE GIRL (only when the shot mentions her or her hands; the SAME woman in every shot): a young woman of about 26, long chestnut-brown hair in a loose low braid over her LEFT shoulder with a few soft strands at the temples. Her CLOTHES are exactly what the shot names. Her HANDS are always the same: slender, smooth, young, soft pale-peach skin, long relaxed fingers, short clean rounded nails with a natural pale-pink polish, no rings, exactly five fingers, natural joints, slow graceful unhurried movements. She is seen from the side or from behind with her head bowed and her eyes on what her hands do; when her face shows it is calm and relaxed, mouth closed, never posing. She never looks into the camera. Never change her hair or face.

PIP THE CAT (only when the shot mentions Pip or the cat): one small, slightly round grey-and-white cat - grey back and head, white chest, white paws and a white blaze on the nose, green eyes, a short tail with a grey tip. Exactly four legs, one tail; moves slowly like a real sleepy cat; never doubled, never morphing. Animals only in clear daylight.

PEOPLE: nobody else is ever seen. The neighbour exists only as the things he leaves on the wall and the smoke from his chimney.

TOOLS: no knives anywhere, no cutting on camera: anything cut is already cut. No precise-hand props.

SOUND: the real, close-miked sound of exactly what happens in the shot, crisp and in sync, as a phone records it from half a metre away (boots on wet gravel, a leek pulled from soil, water from a tap, butter in a pan, a wooden spoon on a pot, rain on glass, a kettle, a robin, a cat's purr) over the soft quiet of the garden or the house. NO music, no words, no singing, no humming.

Natural lived-in realism, not a cartoon, animation, painting, greeting card, advertisement or video-game render. No watermarks, no subtitles, no logos.
