BREATHTAKING NATURE SHOT REQUIREMENTS

One continuous cinematic shot of a real natural place, no cuts, no transitions, no text. {LIGHT}. Real, true-to-life colour and bright, well-exposed footage with open shadows and a clean sky; natural greens, real water colours (glacier turquoise, deep fjord blue, black sand, red rock), no oversaturation, no HDR halos, no fantasy glow, no vignette.

CAMERA: {PACE} The main subject (the peak, waterfall, lake, dune, cliff, glacier or forest named in the brief) stays large and centred, the horizon level in the upper third. The camera never spins, rolls, swings, dives or zooms; no FPV, no whip pan, no orbit faster than a slow walk around the subject.

SHARPNESS (user rule 2026-11-14: a fast drone made the view blurry): every frame is crisp and sharp, shot at a fast shutter like a stabilised gimbal or DJI Mavic at 24 fps: NO motion blur, NO smearing, NO ghosting, NO rolling-shutter wobble, trees, rocks and ripples keep their detail while the camera moves. The ground never rushes past: the nearest object moves across the frame no faster than a slow walking pace, and the move is one steady line with no acceleration.

MOTION: real-time footage, not a time-lapse: clouds, light and shadows stay where they are. Rock, ice, peaks and shorelines stay perfectly rigid; only water, mist, trees in a light breeze, grass, birds and animals move, at their real speed.

SOUND: natural ambience only (water, wind, birds); the music is added in post.

SCREEN: no watermarks, no subtitles, no captions, no logos, no text of any kind, no people posing at the camera.

REAL FOOTAGE: genuine footage as a traveller posts it from their own drone or gimbal camera, never CGI, a render, AI art, a painting or an over-polished postcard.
