# nature-reel

Paths relative to `.claude/skills/nature-reel/`. Rendering rules, server, retries, shot history, music engine, QA and delivery come from `../muse-video/SKILL.md` ("Format skills" shared steps, "Music"). Composing reuses `../place-story/scripts/compose.py` (kinds `plain` = no card, `note` = small yellow kicker + one line over a soft band, inside the feed safe zone).

## Rules (user, 2026-11-14)
1. **Breathtaking, not busy**: one striking natural subject per shot, wide and clean, real colour. At most one short note per shot (kicker = place or fact label, body ≤ 8 words). No fact cards, no hook questions longer than 6 words.
2. **Slow and sharp**: `prompt/nature.txt` forces a walking-pace gimbal move at one constant speed, a fast shutter, no motion blur, no FPV, no orbit. 7 s shots, 3-5 per video, 21-35 s. `scripts/qa.py` motion check must pass; a blurred or time-lapse-looking shot is re-rendered (`RETAKE=1`), never lifted in post. Never use `FAST`.
3. **Six styles, never the same twice in a row** (`styles.json`: serene piano, epic orchestral, indie folk, ambient drift, chill house, earth and drums). `scripts/plan.py` picks one that fits the setting and skips the last 4 used. Each style fixes light, grade, camera moves, music and BPM.
4. **Binge profile**: the visitor lands on the grid and should see nothing but travel. Every post is a nature or place travel video (no other format on the Travel profile); alternate nature-reel and place-story so facts and pure views mix; each video opens on its best view at frame 0 and ends on a frame close to its first so replay is smooth; captions end with a "next" hook ("Next: …") and the pinned top 3 are the strongest nature reels. Post two a day (dashboard slots 07h00 and 19h00), vary style and region between neighbours (never two of the same country or style side by side).
5. **Music**: famous commercial songs and Facebook's Sound Collection cannot be downloaded or embedded (Meta licenses them for in-app use only), so the video carries an **original generated bed** that matches the style (`music.py`, local, no copyright claim). For the famous-sound reach, `plan.py` prints `audio_search` keywords per style: when posting inside the Facebook / Instagram app, search those words in its audio library and pick a trending track there, muting the bed. Never name or imitate a specific copyrighted song in a prompt.

## Steps
0. **Earlier shots**: `../shot-history/scripts/shots.py check` for the place; pick a place not shot before.
1. **Plan**: `python3 scripts/plan.py --place "Milford Sound, New Zealand" --setting fjord > plan.json`. `--style <id>` forces one. Settings it knows: lake forest meadow fjord mountain canyon glacier waterfall valley river coast island lagoon desert dunes savanna.
2. **Shots**: for each plan shot, one line in `jobs.txt`: `LIGHT='<plan light>' MOVE='<plan move>' DURATION=7 scripts/shot "<brief>" ~/Movies/museai/travel-nature-<slug>/s<n>.mp4`. Brief = PLACE (real named spot, its real terrain, vegetation, water colour), a different signature view per shot, best first. Then ONE background call: `../muse-video/scripts/render-queue jobs.txt` (one at a time, 3 accounts at most with `-j`). QA each shot (`../muse-video/scripts/qa.py`); retake blur or drift.
3. **Music**: `uv run --no-project ../muse-video/scripts/music.py --style "<plan music>" --bpm <plan bpm> --body-beats <plan body_beats> --out track.m4a`.
4. **Story + compose**: write `story.json` (`{"place": ..., "beats": [{"kind":"plain","clip":"s1.mp4","secs":6.3}, {"kind":"note","clip":"s2.mp4","title":"MILFORD SOUND","body":"Carved by glaciers","secs":6.3}, ...]}`; shot 1 plain so the view fills frame 0, the others note or plain), then `uv run --no-project --python 3.12 --with pillow --with fonttools python ../place-story/scripts/compose.py --story story.json --music track.m4a --out travel-nature-<slug>.mp4`.
5. **Deliver**: thumbnail (`../thumbnail/scripts/thumb.py travel ...`), captions (`../social-captions/scripts/captions.py check`), then `FINAL=1 CAPTIONS_FILE=... THUMB_DIR=... ../muse-video/scripts/deliver.sh travel-nature-<slug>.mp4 <workdir>`; record the shots (`shots.py record`).
