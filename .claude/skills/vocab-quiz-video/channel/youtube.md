## Description

Can you get 6/6?

Short vocabulary quizzes for language learners. Each Short gives you 6 words in English: guess the word in the target language, then see the answer with its pronunciation and say it back.

Starting with German (A1 to B1, words from the Goethe-Institut lists): every noun with its article, colored by gender (der blue, die red, das green). More languages are coming.

Comment your score (0-6) under every quiz and tell us which topic you want next.
