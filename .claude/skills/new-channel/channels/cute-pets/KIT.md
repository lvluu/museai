Notes: Niche leaders (real-pet compilations, AI 'tiny animal' reels) post 8-20 s loops with natural sound, no talking, close macro framing, a hook in the first second (paw touch, sleepy yawn). Gap: one consistent cozy world (same warm cottage, same palette) and tiny-scale props, so viewers recognise the channel by the first frame. Avoid 'teacup' in the name: it points at teacup-breeding controversy. Never show an animal in distress or danger (comment backlash, platform policy).

## Setup checklist

Accounts are made by you in each app (phone numbers, passwords and sign-up are yours).

- **YouTube:** brand channel. Upload defaults: category, language, *not made for kids*, altered or synthetic
  content = Yes when photoreal AI. One playlist per pillar. Channel trailer = the best first video.
- **Instagram:** professional account, type *Creator*. AI-made: turn on the AI-generated profile label.
  Highlights: what the channel is, how it's made, vote next. Pin the 3 strongest reels.
- **TikTok:** switch to a business or creator account for analytics; AI-generated content label on every
  AI video.
- **Facebook:** a Page, not a profile. Link Instagram in Accounts Center; still post the original file on
  each (no cross-app watermark).
- **X:** media tab works as the portfolio; pin the best video.
- **Threads:** made from Instagram; one topic tag per post.
- **Chinese platforms (小红书, 抖音, B站, 视频号):** no external URL in bio or on screen ("搜 <name>" instead);
  Douyin real-name verification for links and live; set up each in its own app, no region-hopping VPN.

## AI disclosure

- Instagram / Facebook: label every photoreal AI reel (*Add AI label* / *AI info*). Profiles featuring an
  AI-generated person: AI-generated profile label since 2026-08-31.
- YouTube: *altered or synthetic content* for realistic content; tick it when in doubt. Mass-produced,
  repetitive videos are not monetized: every video needs its own idea, shots and sound.
- TikTok: AI-generated content label on realistic AI video; unlabelled ones get removed.
- Say "AI-made" in the bio as well.

## Before day 0

- 5+ finished videos waiting, so the first two weeks never stall.
- Profiles complete on every platform the same day: avatar, banner, bio, AI labels, highlights.
- Times: the dashboard's posting plan for the channel's audience (07:00 and 19:00 GMT+7 is the default test
  plan); after 14 days and ~100 followers, switch to the platform's own *Most active times*
  (`dream-life/scripts/post_time.py --insights`).

## How often

- One post a day while finished videos wait, never two: each post is a test on a seed audience, a second
  one the same day competes with the first.
- When stock runs out, settle at the production rate. Below 3 a week a new account stays unknown; 3-5 a
  week is the floor for growth.
- Experiments (a new look, a new format) go out as Instagram Trial Reels: non-followers only, kept off the
  profile if weak.

## What not to do

- Mix kinds of video on one channel.
- Cross-post a downloaded TikTok (watermark) or re-upload one video with small changes.
- Buy followers, follow/unfollow bots, engagement pods.
- Put a URL on screen or in captions on TikTok and Chinese platforms (throttled).
- Hide that the channel is AI-made.
