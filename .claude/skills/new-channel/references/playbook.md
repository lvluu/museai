# New channel playbook

`kit.py render` copies the "First 30 days" table into KIT.md. Learned from Dream Jobs (dream-life) and the
Panda English China launch; timing details in social-captions `references/posting-times.md`.

## Before day 0
- 5+ finished videos waiting, so the first two weeks never stall.
- Profiles complete on every platform the same day: avatar, banner, bio, AI labels, highlights.
- Times: the dashboard's posting plan for the channel's audience (07:00 and 19:00 GMT+7 is the default test
  plan); after 14 days and ~100 followers, switch to the platform's own *Most active times*
  (`dream-life/scripts/post_time.py --insights`).

## How often
- One post a day while finished videos wait, never two: each post is a test on a seed audience, a second
  one the same day competes with the first.
- When stock runs out, settle at the production rate. Below 3 a week a new account stays unknown; 3-5 a
  week is the floor for growth.
- Experiments (a new look, a new format) go out as Instagram Trial Reels: non-followers only, kept off the
  profile if weak.

## First 30 days
| Days | Do |
|---|---|
| 0 | Profiles, AI labels, banner, highlights on every platform. 3 stories: intro, how it's made, a poll for the first video. |
| 1-15 | One video a day at the plan's time on every platform. Reply to every comment in the first hour. Pin the best 3. |
| 7, 14 | Review each video: 3-second hold, average watch %, sends per reach, follows. Make more of the top 2 hooks; drop the bottom 2. |
| 15-30 | 3-5 a week. One trial post a week testing a new idea type. One collab with a channel in the same niche. |
| 30 | Swap study times for the account's own active times. Keep the cadence that held reach per post; reach per post down > 30 % after raising cadence → go back down. |

## Every post
- Captions per platform: `social-captions`. Cover: `thumbnail` / `travel-thumbnail` in the kit's palette.
- End the caption with one question (comments and sends drive reach).
- The kit's 3-5 topic hashtags, never `#fyp` / `#viral`.

## What not to do
- Mix kinds of video on one channel.
- Cross-post a downloaded TikTok (watermark) or re-upload one video with small changes.
- Buy followers, follow/unfollow bots, engagement pods.
- Put a URL on screen or in captions on TikTok and Chinese platforms (throttled).
- Hide that the channel is AI-made.

## Measure (weekly)
Followers, saves, sends, profile visits, 3-second hold, average watch %. Keep the pillar with the best
saves/views; drop a series after 4 weak posts.
