REAL PHONE FOOTAGE REQUIREMENTS

This must look like a real video a traveller filmed on a modern smartphone and posted, not a film, render or ad. {LIGHT}. Natural phone colours, real dynamic range (bright sky, slightly darker foreground), natural lens, no cinematic letterbox, no colour filter, no HDR halos.

PERSON (only while on screen): one ordinary real traveller, natural skin texture with pores and small imperfections, real hair moving in the wind, casual travel clothes, no make-up look, no model pose. The surprise is genuine and unscripted: eyes and eyebrows, a silent gasp, a hand to the forehead. The head stays up and the eyes stay on the view, amazed, until the camera leaves the face: never bowing the head, closing the eyes or hiding the face. No talking, no words, no lip movement except the gasp. Face, hands and fingers stay anatomically correct and keep the same identity, clothes and hair for the whole clip. The face is evenly lit by the same sunlight from forehead to chin: no grey or flat-coloured patches, boxes, blur squares or masks anywhere on the skin.

CAMERA: the transition always ends pointing exactly where the person was looking; the view is what they faced, never the background behind them. Handheld by a real person, with the tiny natural sway of a hand, never shaky, never jittery. Every move is one fluid motion that eases in and out; the horizon stays level once the view is shown. The final push-in is slow and steady. Any travel (pan, glide) runs at one constant speed between a soft start and a soft stop: no speed ramps, no jerks, no wobble, no direction reversals, no sudden climbs or dives. The clip ends with the camera completely still. The ground, banks and mountains never bend, melt, stretch or morph as the camera moves past them.

VIEW: the real place exactly as it looks, accurate geography and landmarks, real scale, only vegetation that really grows there. Real-time motion: waves, water, mist, leaves and clouds move at true speed, never a time-lapse; the light does not change; rocks, buildings and mountains stay perfectly rigid.

SOUND: natural wind and ambience only (music is added later). No speech, no narration.

SCREEN: no watermarks, no subtitles, no captions, no logos, no phone UI, no text of any kind.
